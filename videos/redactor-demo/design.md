# Redactor demo design

Concept: a quiet laboratory walkthrough, with the actual footage as the evidence.
Palette: paper #f6f7f4, ink #182326, accent #24684a, muted #53665b, rules #dce1dc.
Typography: bundled Source Serif 4 for large editorial statements and Source
Sans 3 for labels and explanations. Both retain their open font license files.
Frame: 1920 x 1080, 80 px safe margins, one strong headline per scene.
Motion: smooth settled entrances and a linear time-progress rule; no visual
effects applied to footage. The comparison is identically cropped and slowed
to 0.5x, explicitly labeled. Deliberate silence suits a live presentation.

The catalog lookup returned no matches after remote item timeouts. Use the
installed spring-pop-entrance and stat-bars-and-fills recipes.
