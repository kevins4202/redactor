{\LARGE\bfseries Redactor}\hfill Midterm report\\[4pt]
{\large Select an object once. Keep it covered as it moves.}\\[3pt]
{\small Draft revised November 19, 2026. Experiments run October 11, 2026.}

We built a tool that tracks a selected object and covers it throughout a video.
The hard part is keeping the whole object covered when it changes size, turns,
or passes behind something. SAM 2 works best on our real videos, but it still misses pixels.

\heading{How it works}
\begin{center}

\heading{What we added}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{.25\linewidth}X@{}}\toprule
\textbf{Started with} & GrabCut segmentation and optical flow that only moved the mask.\\
\textbf{Built / changed} & Scale-aware motion, mask refinement, recovery search, cut checks, multiple targets, corrections, and the review/export UI.\\
\textbf{Integrated} & Pretrained SAM 2 for segmentation and tracking; Grounding DINO for text, face, and plate prompts. We did not train these models.\\
\textbf{Tested} & Original tracker vs. our classical changes vs. SAM 2; box vs. outline prompts; learned recovery; larger coverage margins.\\\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

{\LARGE\bfseries What worked}\par
\heading{Three versions}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{.24\linewidth}X@{}}\toprule
\textbf{1. Fix scale changes} & Fit rotation, translation, and scale from optical flow; refine the mask with GrabCut.\\
\textbf{2. Try to recover} & Search for the original appearance when tracking fails. Flag cuts and uncertain frames.\\
\textbf{3. Try SAM 2} & Use a pretrained video model for propagation; keep the same selection and review workflow.\\\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\textbf{Our classical changes helped the test scenes, but did not carry over well to real video.}
On DAVIS, SAM 2 raised IoU from 0.298 to 0.835 and cut uncovered target pixels from 53.98\% to 2.21\%.
The learned ResNet recovery experiment did not help: DAVIS IoU fell from 0.310 to 0.284.

{\LARGE\bfseries What still needs work}\par
\heading{More coverage helps, but costs background}
\begin{center}\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figures/margin.pdf}\end{center}
\captiontext{Same saved SAM 2 masks on DAVIS; only the added margin changes.
The two percentages have different denominators: target area on the left, whole-frame area on the right.}

At a 16-pixel margin, average uncovered target area drops to 0.74\%, but the worst frame
still leaves \textbf{25.15\%} uncovered. A bigger mask cannot fix a lost target.
SAM 2 also raised no warnings on the crosswalk despite measured misses.

\heading{The next three checks}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{.23\linewidth}Xp{.27\linewidth}@{}}\toprule
\textbf{Problem} & \textbf{Next step} & \textbf{What to measure}\\\midrule
We only scored six crosswalk frames. & Label every frame, then have another person check the labels. & Worst miss and longest uncovered streak.\\
The tracker can miss pixels without warning. & Compare warnings with actual misses on separate clips. & Missed warnings and unnecessary warnings.\\
Recovery struggles after occlusion. & Test repeated exits, reappearances, and similar-looking people. & Wrong-person matches and corrections needed.\\\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\heading{Where we are now}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{.2\linewidth}X@{}}\toprule
\textbf{Working} & Selection, mask preview, multiple targets, corrections, coverage margins, video export, and replay/review logs.\\
\textbf{Still limited} & Identity recovery, warning reliability, face/plate detection recall, and complete coverage. Users still need to review the video.\\\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
